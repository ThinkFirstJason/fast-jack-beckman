About Jack

Racer. Teacher.
Survivor.

Hooked on drag racing at age 7 in the San Fernando Valley, Jack worked his way up from Super Comp to Funny Car. He spent 11 years teaching at Frank Hawley's Drag Racing School, beat lymphoma in 2004, and won his first Funny Car race in Las Vegas two years later. In 2024 he took over the PEAK Chevrolet for John Force — and kept winning.
